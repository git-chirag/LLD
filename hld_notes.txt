Amazon-Specific Advice

For SDE2, interviewers often don't care if your final architecture is perfect.

They care whether you can discuss tradeoffs.

Example:

Instead of saying:

Use Redis.

Say:

I'd use Redis because reads are much more frequent than writes and low latency is important. The downside is cache invalidation and memory cost.

That sounds much more senior.
