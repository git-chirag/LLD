11. Inventory Deep Dive

Most common interviewer question.

Stock:

1 item

Two users buy simultaneously.

Option 1

Optimistic Locking

UPDATE inventory
SET stock=stock-1,
version=version+1
WHERE version=5

Pros:

High throughput

Cons:

Retries
Option 2

Pessimistic Locking

SELECT FOR UPDATE

Pros:

Safe

Cons:

Lower throughput
Option 3

Redis Distributed Lock

Usually not first choice.

Use only if necessary.
