Amazon Interview Sound Bites
Why introduce a queue?

"To decouple services, absorb traffic spikes, improve reliability, and enable asynchronous processing."

Biggest challenge with queues?

"Handling duplicates, retries, ordering, and eventual consistency."

Why Kafka over RabbitMQ?

"Kafka is optimized for high-throughput event streaming and long-term retention, whereas RabbitMQ is optimized for traditional task queues and routing."

Why DLQ?

"To isolate poison messages that repeatedly fail processing and prevent them from blocking the main queue."

Why idempotency?

"Most queue systems provide at-least-once delivery, so consumers must safely handle duplicate messages."
